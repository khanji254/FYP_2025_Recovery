Interim Presentation

Time management – 15 Minutes!

1. Title slide
2. Outline
3. Intro = Background (include image of problem or existing
solution) (1 slide)
4. Problem statement (1 slide)
5. Main objective and specific objectives (1 slide)
6. Literature review – a summary of literature you have looked
at, tabulated. (2 slides max)
7. Gaps identified & possible approaches
8. **Methodology – Max 6 slides – No calculations!!!
9. Results – images, tables. Limit text and explanations (2 slides
max)
10.Budget
11.Conclusion and Way forward
12.Time plan
