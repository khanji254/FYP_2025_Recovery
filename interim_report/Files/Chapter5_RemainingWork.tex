\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This interim report presented the development progress of a reliable dual recovery system for the N4 rocket mission. The work addressed the three core problem areas established in the proposal: telemetry range limitations, uncontrolled deployment-wire heating, and lack of pre-flight status visibility.

\subsection{Summary of Achievements}

The following milestones have been achieved during the interim phase:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Completion of literature review and identification of critical research and implementation gaps relevant to the N4 mission.
    \item Completion of subsystem-level design for the electrical and mechanical architecture, including telemetry, ignition control, feedback monitoring, and deployment hardware.
    \item XBee-PRO 900HP hardware configured and bench-validated via XCTU pre-flight procedure; open-field range testing is scheduled for the next phase.
    \item Successful PWM ignition bench testing demonstrating reliable black-powder ignition at 70\% duty cycle without nichrome burnout under repeated tests.
    \item Proteus BMS simulation validating voltage-monitoring circuit design; hardware accuracy testing pending PCB delivery.
    \item Procurement of major electronic components and progression of PCB/mechanical fabrication activities.
\end{itemize}

These achievements confirm that the design direction selected in the proposal is technically feasible and suitable for mission-level implementation.

\subsection{Interim Findings}

The evidence obtained so far supports three important findings:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The XBee-PRO 900HP platform is suitable for long-range N4 recovery telemetry requirements at the design and bench-configuration level; full range verification at 5~km will be completed during field-testing in the next phase.
    \item PWM-based ignition provides a practical and safer alternative to direct DC dump for nichrome activation.
    \item Integrating continuity and battery-status feedback is essential for launch-inhibit safety logic and operational readiness checks.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Project Status and Next Phase}

At the close of this interim phase, the project has completed design and preliminary subsystem validation, with the next phase focusing on full integration, comprehensive ground testing, and flight-readiness verification. This progression is consistent with the original proposal objectives and keeps the project on track toward a validated N4 dual recovery system.

\subsection{Overall Conclusion}

The interim work demonstrates meaningful technical progress toward a robust and testable recovery system for the N4 rocket. The results to date indicate that the proposed architecture can meet mission performance and safety targets, subject to final integrated-system validation in the remaining project phase.
