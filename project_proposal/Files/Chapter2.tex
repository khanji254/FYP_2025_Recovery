\subsection{Recovery Systems in Student Rocketry}

Recovery systems preserve flight hardware and recorded data, and are therefore mission-critical in university rocketry projects \cite{nakka_recovery, student_rocket_design}. In dual-deployment recovery, a drogue parachute is deployed near apogee for stabilization and drift control, followed by a main parachute at lower altitude for safe touchdown \cite{dual_deploy_analysis, parachute_design}. This staged architecture is widely adopted in high-altitude student missions because it improves landing safety and reduces downrange drift.

\subsubsection{Antenna Link-Budget Justification}

From local N4 antenna justification analysis, the free-space path loss for a 900 MHz link can be modeled using:

\begin{equation}
FSPL(dB) = 20\log_{10}(d) + 20\log_{10}(f) - 147.55
\end{equation}

At $f \approx 915$ MHz and $d=5000$ m, $FSPL \approx 105.66$ dB. The received power estimate is:

\begin{equation}
P_{rx} = P_{tx} + G_{tx} + G_{rx} - FSPL
\end{equation}

For $P_{tx}=24$ dBm and approximately +2 dBi gain at each end, $P_{rx} \approx -77.66$ dBm, yielding a margin of roughly +32 dB relative to a $-110$ dBm sensitivity in long-range mode \cite{xbee_pro_datasheet, pozar_microwave}. This supports the feasibility of the target range under near line-of-sight conditions.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.38\textwidth]{images/duck_antenna.jpg}
\caption{900 MHz duck antenna used in the N4 telemetry configuration}
\label{fig:duck_antenna}
\end{figure}

Dipole antennas exhibit radiation nulls along the antenna axis (the ``donut'' effect), so link quality can degrade sharply when antenna orientation is unfavorable. Practical mitigation includes deliberate ground-station antenna tilt toward the expected trajectory and, where possible, higher-gain directional ground antennas \cite{arrl_antenna_book}.

\subsection{Pyrotechnic Deployment Systems}

Nakka's Crimson Powder research provides baseline context for pyrotechnic charge behavior \cite{nakka_crimson}. Practical student implementations must balance sufficient pressure generation against thermal and structural risks in the recovery bay.

For nichrome-wire ignition, direct battery dump can cause uncontrolled heating, burnout, and low test repeatability. PWM-based drive regulates average resistive-load power:

\begin{equation}
P_{avg} = D\,\frac{V^2}{R}
\end{equation}

where $D$ is duty cycle. This supports controlled thermal ramping and improves reusable pre-flight testing workflows \cite{pwm_ignition_systems, barr_pwm}.

\subsection{Electronic Control Systems}

The ESP32 platform offers dual-core architecture suitable for concurrent state estimation, deployment logic, and telemetry tasks \cite{esp32_datasheet, embedded_flight_computers}. In this project, it also enables deterministic PWM ignition control and integrated health telemetry.

\subsection{Power and Structural Systems}

LiPo batteries provide favorable energy density but require robust management and mechanical retention. High-G launch and deployment events can trigger intermittent connections in insufficiently constrained holders \cite{avionics_vibration}. Therefore, rigid-constrained and vibration-tolerant avionics integration is required.

PETG is frequently selected for functional 3D-printed aerospace support parts because it offers improved ductility and impact tolerance compared with more brittle alternatives, while retaining practical manufacturability. However, mechanical integrity is strongly dependent on layer adhesion, print orientation, and process settings, requiring conservative design and test validation for flight use \cite{petg_mechanical, petg_review_2020, petg_fdm_impact, petg_process_structure}.

\subsection{Identified Research Gaps}

The reviewed literature and Nakuja evidence highlight four unresolved gaps for N4:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item \textbf{Charge-energy quantification gap:} limited N4-specific data linking charge mass to pressure and deployment reliability.
	\item \textbf{Ignition repeatability gap:} limited validation of reusable PWM-controlled nichrome workflows in student-rocketry deployment bays.
	\item \textbf{Telemetry-geometry gap:} strong theoretical link budget can still fail in practice without antenna-null mitigation strategy.
	\item \textbf{Structural validation gap:} PETG avionics support parts are common but often lack shock-profile validation for launch/deployment loads.
\end{enumerate}

These gaps define the contribution scope of this project: robust long-range telemetry, controlled ignition energy delivery, and mechanically resilient avionics integration.
